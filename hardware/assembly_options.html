Build options v2.1: 5 boards, DIY → turnkey

Claude Code Companion Peripheral · re-estimated 2026-07-02 for the all-THT/header BOM (pre-soldered XIAO, mic breakout module, addressable RGB LED). All figures are estimates, not quotes — JLCPCB/PCBWay block automated fetches, so prices come from ~14 triangulated search queries against catalog/forum data. A live quote is the only way to firm these up further.

What changed from the last pass: the old BOM's "zero solder" story rested on a bare LGA mic chip needing reflow — that's gone. Now the open question is different: the XIAO and mic modules (not simple components) may not be placeable by either vendor's standard pick-and-place at all, regardless of price. But that cuts both ways — those 20 through-hole joints are now the easiest part of the build, so full DIY no longer needs hot-air/reflow tooling either. The real lesson: get a real PCBWay quote for the two modules before assuming either "zero solder" or "vendor won't touch it."

A · Full DIY $170–255

5 bare PCBs + every part bought retail; you solder all ~17 components per board.

✓ no reflow/hot-air needed  Effort

You do

  • 20 big, easy THT joints per board for the 2 modules (XIAO + mic breakout) — no fine work at all
  • 5 Choc switches + fine-tip-iron work on the 1 SMD LED and 1 SMD latch switch
  • ~1–2 h/board, ~5–10 h total for 5 — down a lot from the old bare-mic-chip build

Where the money goes

  • PCB fab ~$10–20/5 (board's 104mm length exceeds JLCPCB's $2 bracket)
  • Parts/board: XIAO ~$9–24 · mic breakout ~$7–15 · 5 switches ~$3–4.50 · 5 caps ~$4–4.75 · latch switch ~$1–2 · LED+passives ~$1
  • 3+ supplier shipments ~$20–30

No special tools needed anymore — a fine-tip iron handles everything, including the SMD LED (gull-wing pads, not the old reflow-only mic chip).

B · Vendor solders the fiddly bits only $225–330

Vendor assembles the LED, latch switch, and passives (the small SMD/THT parts) via standard PCBA. You solder the 5 keyswitches and both modules yourself.

✓ beginner-level iron work  Effort

You do

  • ~30 THT joints/board: 5 switches + 20 module pins — all big, easy, no fine work
  • ~20–30 min/board

Vendor does

  • LED, latch switch, passives via standard SMD/THT line
  • Fees: setup+stencil ~$35–70, part-line loading ~$5–10

Worth it mainly if hand-soldering the small SMD LED/latch switch feels intimidating — otherwise Option A gets you most of the way for less.

Recommended

C · Vendor does everything except the 2 modules $245–355

The realistic "best vendor tier" ceiling: keyswitches, latch switch, LED, and passives all vendor-assembled. You solder only the XIAO and mic breakout — 20 big through-hole pins, no fine work whatsoever.

✓ easiest reliable tier  Effort

  • ~10–15 min/board: push 20 pins through, flip, solder
  • Doesn't depend on either vendor agreeing to place an irregular header-mounted module — the one part of this build that's genuinely uncertain is skipped entirely
  • Fees similar to B plus wave/hand-solder labor for the 5 keyswitches (~$3.50 + ~$0.015/joint)

This is the sensible default: it doesn't gamble on an unconfirmed vendor capability, and the 20 joints it leaves you are the easiest ones in the whole design.

Speculative — needs a real quote

D · Full turnkey, zero solder $260–470

PCBWay's consigned/combo parts program explicitly supports customer-supplied parts — in principle you could ship them the XIAO + mic breakout for placement, achieving genuine zero solder. JLCPCB's standard flow likely declines this (no customer-supplied parts outside their library).

? feasibility unconfirmed  Effort

  • Real risk: an EEVblog thread titled "JLCPCB assembly — massive Manual Assembly fee" is a live warning that irregular-part placement fees can blow past expectations — a header-pin module standing 8–10mm off the board is exactly this class of part
  • Only get quoted, don't order, until PCBWay's sales team confirms they'll actually take the job and at what fee
  • If the quote comes back reasonable, this is genuinely zero-solder; if not, fall back to C — the switches/passives/LED work is identical either way

E · Whole device, one vendor $285–430

Option C (the reliable tier) plus 5 sets of enclosure shells from JLC3DP or PCBWay's print service, in one combined order. STL/STEP files already in hardware/enclosure/output/.

✓ one order, easy self-assembly  Effort

  • Shell pairs est. $5–12 each in gray resin/PA12
  • Your entire build: solder 2 modules (10–15 min) + 4 screws + 5 keycaps per device
  • Swap in D's modules-included cost if that quote comes back reasonable

Sensible after the layout sign-off (issue #12) and one fit-check print — the enclosure grew to 22.6mm thick this revision, worth confirming in hand before ordering 5 sets.

Caveats & the one real next step

  • Routing still doesn't change this math — assembly cost keys off board size, layer count, and part count, all fixed. The board is placed + netlisted, not routed (issue #13).
  • New this pass: the C&K PCM12SMTR latch switch is listed Obsolete by one distributor — still orderable elsewhere at this writing, but pick a confirmed-active replacement before finalizing (issue #16). Not reflected as a cost swing above since a replacement part's price is unknown.
  • XIAO pre-soldered pricing varies widely by source/region (£19 UK retail vs. likely cheaper direct-from-Seeed US pricing) — get one real quote rather than trusting either end of that range.
  • Option D's range is wide on purpose — module-placement fees are the one thing search snippets categorically cannot verify. Don't treat either end of $260–470 as a real number.

Bottom line: pick C as the default — it doesn't gamble on unconfirmed vendor capability, and what it leaves you to solder (2 modules, 20 big pins) is genuinely the easiest part of the build. Get a real PCBWay quote for D before ruling it out; upgrade to E once the enclosure fit-check print is done.
